PepsiCo — Doritos That’s Nuts

Home | Case Study

Agency: TAG · Client: PepsiCo · Role: Creative Art Director · Product: Doritos That’s Nuts — Chilli Heatwave

Big flavour. A new product expression.

The challenge

Bring Doritos’ bold personality into a different snack format while making the product immediately understandable.

The creative challenge was to balance three layers of recognition: the established Doritos identity, the expressive That’s Nuts branding, and the Chilli Heatwave flavour. Each needed a clear role without overwhelming the pack.

The creative idea

Make the attitude unmistakable. Make the product clear.

The art direction brings together oversized typography, a confident red palette, and a close-up product visual. The combination communicates the energy of the brand while helping audiences understand what’s inside: flavour-coated peanuts.

Solving the visual hierarchy

With multiple brand elements sharing one surface, hierarchy became the central design problem.

The Doritos logo establishes familiarity. The oversized That’s Nuts lettering gives the product its own personality. The flavour descriptor and peanut visual complete the story, connecting the name with a tangible taste experience.

The challenge was making these elements work together at a glance.

Directing the product story

As Creative Art Director, my focus was on how branding, typography, colour, and product imagery worked as one composition.

The product visual plays a particularly important role. Showing both the coated exterior and the peanut inside helps explain the snack, while the textured surface adds appetite appeal.

The background pattern adds energy and depth, supporting the main identity without competing with it.

Building clarity for communications

The beauty-pack approach strips away secondary information to foreground the core visual assets. This creates a focused foundation for communications, keeping the brand, product name, flavour, and product visual prominent.

It also sets a clear principle for adaptation: preserve the hierarchy so the product remains recognisable as the format changes.

The takeaway

This project demonstrates my approach to creative art direction: balancing a bold brand expression with the clarity needed to introduce a different product format. Every element has a job—to build recognition, communicate flavour, or make the product more desirable.
