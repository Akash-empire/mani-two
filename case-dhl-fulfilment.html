DHL Fulfilment — Make Room for What’s Next

Home | Case Study

The challenge

Make fulfilment relevant to growing e-commerce businesses by connecting operational support with the ambition behind every order.

The creative challenge was to turn a complex service into a clear, engaging story that business owners could recognise in their own working lives.

The audience insight

Growth is exciting—until keeping up with orders starts taking time away from building the business.

For founders, fulfilment affects more than daily operations. It shapes how much attention they can give to developing products, connecting with customers, and planning their next step.

The big idea

Make Room for What’s Next.

More room to create. More room to connect. More room to grow.

The idea connects fulfilment with a meaningful business benefit: space to focus on the future.

My creative direction

Working at TAG as Creative Art Director, I shaped the creative approach around the tension between growing ambition and limited capacity.

The direction puts founders and their businesses at the centre of the story. Familiar moments—stock filling a workspace, orders interrupting creative work, packing extending into the evening—make the challenge tangible.

Confident headlines and DHL’s yellow and red palette give the message a clear brand presence. Graphic elements inspired by parcels, labels, and movement connect the human story to the service.

Creative problem solving

The toughest challenge was balancing simplicity with substance. The campaign needed to communicate an immediate benefit while supporting a more detailed conversation about fulfilment.

The solution was a flexible messaging system built around specific business pressures. Each execution introduces a recognisable challenge, connects it to the wider growth story, and gives the audience a clear next step.

This approach allows the central idea to remain consistent while the message changes across the customer journey.

Connecting the campaign

The platform gives awareness communications a strong emotional starting point, while more detailed content explains the solution.

Headlines such as “Your next big idea needs more than a packing table” translate operational pressure into an accessible story. Supporting content carries that interest towards relevant service information and an enquiry.

The intended impact

Strengthen awareness and consideration of DHL Fulfilment among growing e-commerce businesses, with qualified website visits, content engagement, and sales enquiries as relevant measures of success.

The takeaway

This project demonstrates my approach to creative art direction: finding the human story within a complex service and building a clear visual and messaging system around it.
